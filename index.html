Argument diagrammer

Numbered circles, arrows, brackets. Export as PNG for slides and handouts.

Circles

Ranges like 1-5 work. Double-click a circle to rename it.

Connect

Drag the dot under a circle onto another circle to draw an arrow toward it. Select two or more circles, then Bracket. The bracket gets its own dot.

Unbracket turns a bracket back into separate arrows. Unarrow removes the selected arrow, or every arrow between the selected circles. Auto-align lays out the selected circles (or everything, with nothing selected) in even rows, conclusion at the bottom.

Keys

Shift+click adds to the selection. Drag on empty space to select a group.

B bracket   U unbracket   F flip   X unarrow

L auto-align   A align row   Delete remove   Esc deselect

Ctrl+Z undo   arrow keys nudge the selected circles

Selecting premises and a lower circle together, then pressing Bracket, draws the bracket and its arrow in one step.
